### Known bias - not for publication

Do not publish these numbers. Two known problems remain:

1. **Elo seeding bias.** The seeding pins leagues first ingested in 2018-19 (nl, pt, tr, gr, be, sco) near 1500. Their clubs underperform Elo expectation against Big-5 clubs in every season since 2019-20, by about 0.06 to 0.22 expected score per match, which is roughly 70 Elo points of overrating. That is why PSV, Porto and Sporting rank near Real Madrid in the pre-season table.
2. **Over-concentration at the top.** Bayern's 29.7% pre-season title share (29.1% after this run's data refresh) reflects concentration with no rating uncertainty: ratings are treated as exact in the simulation.

Both are scheduled for a calibration step that must pass before any numbers are published.
