## Sanity note

Both runs use elo-dc-v2.1: 50,000 runs, seed 1, 1.3 s (md00) and 1.1 s (live); footer sums are Top 8 = 8.00, Top 24 = 24.00, Win = 1.00. Every md00 Top 8 and Top 24 probability is strictly inside (0,1). The live table has 72 of 144 league-phase fixtures played; several teams are at 100.0% Top 24 or near 0%, which is expected at the halfway point.

### Known limitations

1. **Residual league bias.** Mid-league clubs still underperform Big-5 clubs in the final predictions: -0.078 expected score per match under v2.1 (v1: -0.139).
2. **League-level pooling.** With g = 0.9 and no decay, a whole league's level moves with each cross-league European result, so clubs inherit their compatriots' European record (for example Lille and Lens at md00).
3. **Exact ratings.** Ratings are treated as exact in the simulation (sigma = 0; no sigma improved the round-1 tournament check), so title shares may be concentrated.

Details: [G4 context in reports/v21-evaluation.md](v21-evaluation.md).
